FAQ

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form below with your event date, city, and the rental items you are interested in. We confirm availability and details before booking.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery and setup are part of the quote process. Final delivery availability depends on distance, event timing, and rental items.

Can I pick up rental items myself?

Some items may qualify for customer pickup. Larger items, equipment requiring setup, or safety-sensitive items may require delivery and setup.

Are the product photos final?

No. This first website version uses placeholders on purpose. Replace them with real inventory photos as equipment is finalized.
