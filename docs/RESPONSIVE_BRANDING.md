# Responsive Regal Rentals identity

The high-detail owner-approved shield in `assets/regal-official-shield.webp`
remains the **full-color master** for large promotional placements.

For small placements, use `assets/regal-compact-rr.svg`. It keeps the
royal-purple shield, gold crown, and mirrored RR, removing the small
unreadable words from the icon. The SVG is a **simplified visual variant**,
not a new business identity; the original full artwork is unchanged.

On the storefront, the name appears beside the crest as a readable two-line
wordmark (Regal / RENTALS). The compact crest is used for the site header,
site footer, browser favicon, admin navigation, payment/signing navigation,
and small legacy rental-brand placements.

The original full shield continues to appear on the large admin login
and large promotional artwork. The shop and booking workflows are untouched.
