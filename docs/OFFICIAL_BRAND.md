# Official Regal Rentals identity — October 2026

The approved master artwork is the single Regal Rentals shield selected by the two owners.
Do not redraw the RR monogram, crown, the words REGAL or RENTALS, or the shield.

## Website and admin
The official optimized shield lives at `/assets/regal-official-shield.webp`.
It is used for the homepage header/footer, the admin login/header, payment,
signing, legacy rental, 404 screens, and browser icons. The old competing
shield files remain unreferenced to simplify rollback and are not the active
identity.

The web file is an optimized small-size export of the approved user-supplied
transparent artwork, sized for page chrome and icons; it is **not** the
high-resolution print master. Do not use the small web export for signage.

## Full-production master pack
The owner receives a separate downloadable master kit containing the original
1254 px transparent PNG, large exports, a reduced-color small-format design,
black/white/purple monochrome, an automatically traced single-color SVG,
social avatar squares, a Facebook cover, and horizontal lockups.

The automatically traced SVG is *not* a manually redrawn vector master.
Use vendor proofs for embroidery, engraving or vinyl cutting before a large run,
because very fine text, crown details and strokes may not survive small sizes.

## Colors and photography
The dominant identity is regal purple and gold. Use a restrained light backdrop
on the retail site so the shield remains the focal point. The site body and
interactive checkout/inventory/booking behaviors should not be changed merely
to adopt the identity.

Social account profile and cover images are exported as downloadable images;
the site repository does not have permission to edit those external accounts.
