# Payment Security Policy

Effective with generated customer agreement Version 2.5:

- Credit-card payment: no refundable security deposit. The same valid credit card used for payment must be securely stored on file before equipment release and may be charged for documented amounts permitted by the rental agreement.
- Debit-card payment: refundable security deposit equal to exactly 50% of the final confirmed rental subtotal.
- Cash payment: refundable security deposit equal to exactly 50% of the final confirmed rental subtotal.
- The 50% deposit has no minimum and no maximum. It is rounded only to the nearest cent when mathematically necessary.
- The deposit is payment security, not a damage waiver, and does not limit the customer's responsibility.
- Bookings over $1,500 or containing unpriced items require staff review before equipment release, but the deposit formula remains 50% of the final confirmed subtotal.
- The website records the customer's selected payment method and corresponding security requirement with the signed agreement. Payment collection, secure card storage, and refunds still require an integrated payment processor.
